title: "Portland Victims' Memorial: Human Lives are More Than Statistics. "
intro: >-
  The Portland Victims' Memorial honors people who have been killed or suffered
  life-changing injuries in traffic crashes on Portland streets. Through their
  names, photographs, and stories, we remember the lives behind the statistics
  and the families, friends, and communities forever changed by traffic
  violence.


  This memorial is not a comprehensive record. It grows as families and loved ones share stories with us, and some people may appear more than once when we receive tributes from different loved ones.\
