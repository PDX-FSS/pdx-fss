Fallon Smart, a 15-year-old incoming Franklin High sophomore, was crossing SE Howthorne and 43rd when she was  killed in a hit-and-run crash by a speeding driver. Fallon was bright and engaged. She was deeply loved by her family, friends, and community. The at-fault driver, a Saudi national, cut off his ankle monitor and fled the U.S. before trial, prompting the federal government to adopt the "Fallon Smart Policy" targeting foreign officials who aid fugitives.
